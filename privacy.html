Privacy Policy

rclone homelab · last updated 20 October 2026

1. Who operates the app

rclone homelab is operated by Frank, a private individual, for his own household. Contact: franksecops@icloud.com. The app has exactly one user: the operator's own Google account.

2. Which Google data the app accesses

The app requests only the Google Drive scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. With this scope the app can see and change only files and folders that the app itself has created in the user's Google Drive. It cannot see, read, change or delete any other file in Google Drive, and it does not access Gmail, contacts, calendar or any other Google service.

The app does not request the user's name, email address or profile.

3. How the data is used

The app is used for two purposes only:

  • to upload and maintain an encrypted backup of the operator's own files, and
  • to keep a read-only copy of the operator's own document archive in a dedicated folder.

The files belong to the operator. The app reads the files it created only to check, update or restore the backup.

4. Storage and sharing

  • The OAuth token is stored only on the operator's private home server, in a file readable only by the operator's account. It is never published or sent anywhere else.
  • Data received from Google APIs is not shared, sold or transferred to any third party, not used for advertising, and not used to train any AI or machine-learning model.
  • No analytics, tracking or cookies are used.

5. Retention and deletion

Backups are kept according to the operator's own retention settings and are deleted by the operator. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; after revocation the app can no longer access Google Drive.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
